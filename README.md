player.py: where the player's initial stats, weapon types, stats panel, and inventory system are. its used to calculate players damage dealt and damage taken from the enemy based on the player's stat from its initial stat, stat points that they put in different stats and the stats that the weapon giving.

battle.py: system of the battle, use to detemine whether player is win or lose, as well as the reward that player have a chance to get after player wining the lvl/stage

game.py: use to start the game, it has main menu where u can create new game or continue playing from where you left off, win menu(player can choose whether save the game and leave or going to next lvl) and lose menu(player can either retry or restart the game)
